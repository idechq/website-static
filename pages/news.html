iDEC Summer Webinar 2026: Light-Directed Evolution of Protein Switches

Aug 21 2026

The iDEC Summer Webinar returns on Tuesday, 25 August 2026, 13:00 GMT+1. Dr Vojislav Gligorovski (École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne) will present Light-Directed Evolution of Protein Switches. Writing in Cell, he introduced Optovolution, a light-driven selection strategy that evolves proteins for dynamic, multi-state and computational behaviour rather than a single static phenotype. The webinar is free and open to the public. Researchers and students in directed evolution, synthetic biology and origin-of-life research are welcome to join. The speaker will take questions live. Register today by scanning the QR code!

iDEC 2026 Festival Rescheduled to 7–8 November in Cambridge

Apr 25 2026

Dear participating teams and partners,

Since the launch of iDEC 2026, we have seen strong interest and engagement from the global community. As an international competition focused on biological design and research practice, iDEC aims to provide a high-quality platform for academic exchange and a well-structured experience for participating teams.

Based on a review of the global academic calendar and feedback from teams, and to improve overall participation and accessibility, the Organizing Committee has decided to adjust the Festival schedule:

The iDEC Festival, originally planned for 24–25 October, 2026 in Cambridge, will now take place on 7–8 November, 2026.

Reasons for the Adjustment

Autumn has become a busy period for academic events in biotechnology, especially in Europe. This adjustment is intended to better fit into the broader academic schedule and make participation more manageable for teams.

We hope the new dates will make it easier for teams from different regions to attend the Festival in Cambridge. After completing their presentations and evaluations at iDEC, teams may also find it more convenient to attend and enjoy other academic events happening around the same time, helping to make the most of their travel.

The updated timeline also provides additional time for project development and supports a more consistent and thorough evaluation process.

Support for teams

We understand that this change may affect existing plans. The iDEC Organizing Committee will support registered teams in adjusting their arrangements, including providing updated invitation letters and relevant documentation if needed.

Thank you for your understanding and continued support. We look forward to welcoming you to Cambridge this November.

iDEC HQ
